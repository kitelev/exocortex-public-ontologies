object: "\" The $\\textit{standard cubic metre per minute}$ (scmm) is the molar flow rate of a gas (such as natural gas) contained in a volume of one cubic metre at reference temperature and pressure conditions. As such, it is a measure of the actual amount of gas, not the volume of the gas. The reference conditions for standard cubic metre per minute are 0 degrees Celsius and 101.325 kilopascals (kPa) of pressure. \"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
