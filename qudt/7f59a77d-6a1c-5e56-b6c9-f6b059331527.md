One lumen represents the total flux of light emitted, equal to the intensity in candelas multiplied by the solid angle in steradians into which the light is emitted. A full sphere has a solid angle of $4\\cdot\\pi$ steradians. A light source that uniformly radiates one candela in all directions has a total luminous flux of $1 cd\\cdot 4 \\pi sr = 4 \\pi cd \\cdot sr \\approx 12.57 \\; \\text{lumens}$. \"Lumen\" is a Latin word for light.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
