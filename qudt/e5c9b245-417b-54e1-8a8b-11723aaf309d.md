It is the volume of fluid (in square metres) that can pass through a material in one second under a pressure difference of one bar. This unit is particularly important in fields such as petrophysics, hydrogeology, and materials engineering, where understanding the flow of fluids through porous media is crucial. A $Bar$ is not an SI unit but is a unit of pressure that is widely used in various scientific and engineering contexts.
