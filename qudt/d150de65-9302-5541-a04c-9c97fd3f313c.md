In utility alternating-current (AC) and audio-frequency (AF) circuits, capacitors with values on the order of $1 \\mu F$ or more are common. At radio frequencies (RF), a smaller unit, the picofarad (pF), is often used.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
