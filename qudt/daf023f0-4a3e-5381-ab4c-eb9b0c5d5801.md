object: "\"A barad is a dyne per square centimetre ($dyn \\cdot cm^{-2}$), and is equal to $0.1 Pa $ ($1 \\, micro \\, bar$, $0.000014504 \\, p.s.i.$). Note that this is precisely the microbar, the confusable bar being related in size to the normal atmospheric pressure, at $100\\,dyn \\cdot cm^{-2}$. Accordingly barad was not abbreviated, so occurs prefixed as in $cbarad = centibarad$. Despite being the coherent unit for pressure in c.g.s., barad was probably much less common than the non-coherent bar. Barad is sometimes called $barye$, a name also used for $bar$.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
