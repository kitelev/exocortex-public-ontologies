It is defined as one twelfth of the rest mass of an unbound atom of carbon-12 in its nuclear and electronic ground state, and has a value of $1.660538782(83) \\times 10^{-27} kg$.  One $Da$ is approximately equal to the mass of one proton or one neutron. The CIPM have categorised it as a $\\textit{\"non-SI unit whose values in SI units must be obtained experimentally\"}$.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
