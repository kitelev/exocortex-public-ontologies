The inverse of the $\\textit{Planck Function}$ is used to find the $\\textit{Brightness Temperature}$ of an object. The precise formula for the Planck Function depends on whether the radiance is determined on a $\\textit{per unit wavelength}$ or a $\\textit{per unit frequency}$. In the ISO System of Quantities, $\\textit{Planck Function}$ is defined by the formula: $Y = -G/T$, where $G$ is Gibbs Energy and $T$ is thermodynamic temperature.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
