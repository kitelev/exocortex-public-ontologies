For slow walking (just over two miles per hour), $3.0\\,kilo\\,calorie\\,per\\,hour\\,per\\,kilogram$. For fast steady running (about 10 miles per hour), $16.3\\,kilo\\,calorie\\,per\\,hour\\,per\\,kilogram$. Females expend about 10 per cent less energy than males of the same size doing a comparable activity.
