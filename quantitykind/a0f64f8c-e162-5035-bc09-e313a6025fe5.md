object: "\"The inclination to each other of two intersecting lines, measured by the arc of a circle intercepted between the two lines forming the angle, the center of the circle being the point of intersection.  An acute angle is less than $90^\\circ$, a right angle $90^\\circ$; an obtuse angle, more than $90^\\circ$ but less than $180^\\circ$; a straight angle, $180^\\circ$; a reflex angle, more than $180^\\circ$ but less than $360^\\circ$; a perigon, $360^\\circ$. Any angle not a multiple of $90^\\circ$ is an oblique angle. If the sum of two angles is $90^\\circ$, they are complementary angles; if $180^\\circ$, supplementary angles; if $360^\\circ$, explementary angles.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
