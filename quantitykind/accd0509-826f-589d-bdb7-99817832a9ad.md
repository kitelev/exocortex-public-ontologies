object: "\"The heat capacity ratio, or ratio of specific heats, is the ratio of the heat capacity at constant pressure ($C_P$) to heat capacity at constant volume ($C_V$). For an ideal gas, the heat capacity is constant with temperature ($\\theta$). Accordingly we can express the enthalpy as $H = C_P*\\theta$ and the internal energy as $U = C_V \\cdot \\theta$. Thus, it can also be said that the heat capacity ratio is the ratio between enthalpy and internal energy.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
