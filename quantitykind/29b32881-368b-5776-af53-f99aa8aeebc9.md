Gain is usually expressed in decibels and is widely used to denote transducer gain.  An increase or amplification. In radar there are two general usages of the term: (a) antenna gain, or gain factor, is the ratio of the power transmitted along the beam axis to that of an isotropic radiator transmitting the same total power; (b) receiver gain, or video gain, is the amplification given a signal by the receiver.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
