object: "\" The quantity kind $\\textit{Electric Conductivity}$ or $\\textit{Specific Conductance}$ is a measure of a material's ability to conduct an electric current. When an electrical potential difference is placed across a conductor, its movable charges flow, giving rise to an electric current. The conductivity $\\sigma$ is defined as the ratio of the electric current density $J$ to the electric field, $E$: $J = \\sigma E$. In isotropic materials, conductivity is scalar-valued, however in general, conductivity is a tensor-valued quantity. \"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
