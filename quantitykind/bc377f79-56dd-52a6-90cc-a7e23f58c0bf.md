A magnetic circuit almost acts as though the flux is \"conducted\", therefore permeance is larger for large cross sections of a material and smaller for longer lengths. This concept is analogous to electrical conductance in the electric circuit.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
