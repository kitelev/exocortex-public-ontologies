It is a physical value that can be used to determine the type and properties of a material (e.g. soil or snow). It has a particular importance for adsorption, heterogeneous catalysis, and reactions on surfaces.\"^^[[ce59db63-4c64-5bd2-9f95-1e220f8b4891]]"
